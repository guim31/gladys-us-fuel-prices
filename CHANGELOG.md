# Changelog

All notable changes to this integration are documented in this file. The format
follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project
uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

Describe each change under `## [Unreleased]` as you make it. The Release
workflow moves that section under the version it ships, and the section becomes
the notes of the version's GitHub Release.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Weekly average gasoline and diesel prices of the United States, from the
  EIA Open Data API v2: the U.S. average, the PADD regions, 9 states and 10
  cities, one device per area.
- For each fuel (Regular, Midgrade, Premium, Diesel): the price in $/gal, the
  change over one week and over one year, and the survey date.
- Scene trigger "New weekly fuel prices (EIA)", with a ready-made summary.
- Dashboard widget with the prices of any area and their changes.
- "Test the connection to EIA" action.
- Documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
